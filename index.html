Invisible characters
Zero-width spaces, the narrow no-break space (U+202F) reported in chatbot output, soft hyphens, fillers. Joiners that do a real job in emoji, Persian or Hindi are left alone.

      
Smuggled messages
Text hidden in Unicode tag characters, in variation selectors after an emoji, or in zero-width binary. Decoded and shown, and critical when it is an instruction.

      
Hidden prompts
White-on-white or 1px text, display:none, comments and alt text in pages, Markdown and Word files that talk to a model instead of a reader.

      
Trojan Source
Bidirectional controls that make code read differently from how it runs (CVE-2021-42574), and the right-to-left override in file names.

      
Lookalike letters
A Cyrillic а in pаypal.com, mixed-script identifiers, 𝐬𝐭𝐲𝐥𝐞𝐝 letters that slip past filters. Real Russian or Greek text is not reported.

      
What it cannot do
Statistical watermarks such as SynthID live in which words a model chose, not in characters. No character cleaner can see or remove them, and unseen does not pretend to.
